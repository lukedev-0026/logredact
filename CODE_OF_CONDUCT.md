# Code of conduct

logredact is meant to be a welcoming project for people of every background
and level of experience.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Discuss ideas and code, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a change, and explain your own
  decisions when you decline something.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes and language.
- Personal or political attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions after being asked to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and
any other place where someone represents the project.

Report problems to the maintainer at luke.taaffe0026@gmail.com. Reports are
kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may
temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code.
